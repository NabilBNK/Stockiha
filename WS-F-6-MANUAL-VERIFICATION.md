Install the new build and check that the screen shows `WS-F-6.0`. Sign in as Admin.

1. Open a cash session with a float of 2,000. The cash session screen shows a "Sales in this session" section that says there are no sales yet.
2. At the till, sell product A × 2 in cash. Note A's stock quantity before and after.
3. Back on the cash session screen, the sale appears in the list with a "Cancel sale" button.
4. Click Cancel sale and choose "Custom reason" with no description. It must refuse. Choose "Wrong item" and confirm.
5. A cancellation slip prints on your receipt printer, showing "ANNULATION DE VENTE", an AN- number and the original sale number.
6. The sale now shows "Cancelled (AN-…)" and has no button. A's stock is back to its quantity before the sale.
7. Try to cancel the same sale again. There is no button, so it cannot be cancelled twice.
8. Make a cash sale of 500 with a 100 DA discount, then cancel it.
9. Close the session and count exactly 2,000 (the float, since both sales were cancelled). It closes with no difference.
10. Open a new session. Make a credit sale to a customer with no other debt. Note the customer's balance, cancel the sale, and check that the balance goes back to what it was.
11. Make another credit sale to that customer, record a payment of part of it from the Customers page, then try to cancel the sale. It must refuse and tell you to refund the payment first.
12. Open Documents. The cancelled sale shows as Cancelled, and an "Annulation de vente" document exists. Open Journals: the cancellation has an entry that mirrors the sale's entry.
13. Turn receipt printing OFF in Settings, then cancel a sale. No slip prints, and "Print slip again" is still offered.
14. Close the session. The cancelled sales can no longer be cancelled from anywhere.
15. Switch to French, then Arabic. The section and the dialog read correctly, and Arabic is right-to-left.
